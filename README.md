# How much revenue is hiding behind order cancellations and refunds? 

I analyzed a dataset of 3,000 global transactions to evaluate sales drivers, regional performance, and revenue leakage across multiple fulfillment channels.

## Key Metrics & High-Level Findings:

•  Gross Revenue Analyzed: $585,298.89 across 3,000 total orders.

 • Revenue at Risk (28.3%): $165,601.85 was lost to cancellations ($77.2k) and refunds ($88.4k), resulting in a 70.8% completed order rate.
 
•  Top Revenue Driver: Electronics generated $265,519.17 (~45.4% of total sales), led by            the 4K Action Camera ($124,141.71).

•  Geographic Standouts: Asia Pacific ($176,694.39) and North America ($145,102.81) led regional sales.

•  Balanced Channels: Sales were evenly split across Direct B2B, Retail Partners, Marketplaces, and Online platforms (~$140k–$148k each).

## Tools Used:

•   Excel & Pivot Tables: Data cleaning, KPI modeling, and revenue leakage calculations.

•   Data Visualization: Executive dashboards tracking monthly performance, product performance, and customer segmentation.
